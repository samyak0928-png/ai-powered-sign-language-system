INTELLIGENT FUSION

Sensor Fusion: Finger Configuration + Hand Motion

Neither flex sensors nor an IMU alone can fully recognize sign language. Sensor fusion combines both streams into a rich spatial-temporal representation.

🔬

Why Sensor Fusion Matters

Many signs share identical finger shapes (e.g. static fists) but differ in wrist rotation or vertical movement. By fusing 5 flex channels with 6 IMU axes into an 11-dimensional feature vector, the recognition model achieves unambiguous sign discrimination.

Flex Sensors (x5)

Finger Bend Detection

Carbon/conductive polymer strip sensors along each finger that change electrical resistance proportionally to curvature angle (0° straight to 90° fully bent).

6-DOF IMU Sensor

Hand Orientation & Motion

Wrist-mounted MPU-6050 / LSM6DS3 accelerometer and gyroscope measuring 3D spatial velocity, roll, pitch, and yaw for dynamic sign recognition.

ESP32 Microcontroller

Signal Acquisition & Edge

32-bit dual-core MCU digitizing analog sensor voltages through multi-channel ADCs, applying baseline debouncing, and formatting telemetry packets.

Bluetooth Low Energy

Wireless Telemetry

High-efficiency BLE 5.0 streaming telemetry data packets wirelessly to companion devices with minimal latency (<20ms) and ultra-low power consumption.

Rechargeable LiPo Battery

Portable Power

Compact 3.7V 500mAh lithium polymer battery with integrated TP4056 charge controller and voltage regulation supporting all-day wearable usage.
