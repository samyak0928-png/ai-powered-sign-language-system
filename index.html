HARDWARE ANATOMY

Inside the Smart Glove

An overview of the physical sensing, computing, and power modules intended for the wearable glove architecture.

Flex Sensors (x5)

Finger Bend Detection

Carbon/conductive polymer strip sensors along each finger that change electrical resistance proportionally to curvature angle (0° straight to 90° fully bent).

6-DOF IMU Sensor

Hand Orientation & Motion

Wrist-mounted MPU-6050 / LSM6DS3 accelerometer and gyroscope measuring 3D spatial velocity, roll, pitch, and yaw for dynamic sign recognition.

ESP32 Microcontroller

Signal Acquisition & Edge

32-bit dual-core MCU digitizing analog sensor voltages through multi-channel ADCs, applying baseline debouncing, and formatting telemetry packets.

Bluetooth Low Energy

Wireless Telemetry

High-efficiency BLE 5.0 streaming telemetry data packets wirelessly to companion devices with minimal latency (<20ms) and ultra-low power consumption.

Rechargeable LiPo Battery

Portable Power

Compact 3.7V 500mAh lithium polymer battery with integrated TP4056 charge controller and voltage regulation supporting all-day wearable usage.

USER ADAPTATION

Every Hand Is Different. The System Must Adapt.

Hand size, finger length, sensor elasticity, and signing speed vary per individual. A planned 3-step calibration normalizes individual baseline variances.

01

Calibrate

The user performs 3 simple baseline poses (open palm, flat rest, and tight fist) to record anatomical minimum and maximum resistance bounds.

02

Normalize

Raw voltage telemetry is dynamically mapped onto a standardized 0.0–1.0 range using user-specific min-max calibration coefficients.

03

Personalize

Fine-tuning weights adapt to the signer's individual movement cadence, reducing false negatives and maintaining high recognition confidence.

ENGINEERING TRADE-OFFS

Why a Smart Glove? Why Not Just a Camera?

An objective, balanced comparison of wearable sensor technology versus computer vision approaches.

Evaluation Dimension Smart Glove Sensing (This Concept) Camera / Computer Vision Alone
Lighting Independence 100% immune to dark rooms, direct sunlight, or shadows Prone to degradation in low-light or uneven illumination
Occlusion & Line-of-Sight Direct physical contact; finger flexion always measured Fingers occluded behind palms or bodies are lost by single camera
Privacy & Mobility No video streaming required; sign while walking or outdoors Requires pointing camera at user; raises privacy concerns in public
Facial & Torso Grammar Glove alone does not measure eyebrows or facial cues Naturally captures facial expressions and head nods
Wearable Convenience Requires donning a physical glove Zero wearables required; uses standard smartphone camera
⚡

Long-Term Ideal: Glove + Camera Sensor Fusion

The optimal future architecture combines glove-based finger bending & IMU dynamics with lightweight camera-based facial expression tracking for comprehensive natural sign language comprehension.

The Solution: Forward-Directional Wearable Bridge

  • ▸ Directional Focus: Sign Language User → Non-Sign Language Listener.
  • ▸ Smart Glove: Captures finger flexion (flex sensors) and hand trajectory (6-DOF IMU).
  • ▸ AI Classifier: Interprets temporal movement vectors into recognized sign glosses.
  • ▸ NLP Synthesis: Converts sign glosses into natural spoken and written sentences.
Pipeline Architecture

SIGN → SMART GLOVE → SENSORS → AI INFERENCE → NLP GRAMMAR → TEXT + SPEECH

How It Works: Hardware & AI Intelligence

  • ▸ Flex Sensing: 5 carbon tracks measure individual finger bend percentages (0-100%).
  • ▸ IMU 6-DOF: Captures 3D acceleration (X/Y/Z) and angular rotation (Roll/Pitch/Yaw).
  • ▸ ESP32 Edge Sampling: Digitizes at 50Hz and transmits via Bluetooth Low Energy.
  • ▸ Personalization: 3-step calibration normalizes user-specific anatomical variations.
