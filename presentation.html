SLIDE 01 / 06 • THE PROBLEM

The Communication Barrier Is Asymmetric

Over 70 million Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals worldwide face daily barriers when interacting with a hearing world that rarely understands sign language.

⚡

Healthcare & Triage

In emergencies, delayed communication can result in misdiagnosis, anxiety, and preventable medical crises.

🏬

Daily Independence

Purchasing groceries, asking for transit directions, or banking requires reliance on written notes or interpreters.

💼

Workplace Equity

Exclusion from real-time spontaneous conversations restricts career progression and social inclusion.

SLIDE 02 / 06 • THE SOLUTION

Forward Directional Communication

The user signs naturally. The wearable smart glove captures movement. AI recognizes gestures and synthesizes acoustic speech directed outward toward the listener.

1. SIGNER → 2. SMART GLOVE → 3. AI ENGINE → 4. NLP LAYER → 5. SPEECH OUT

Unlike camera-only apps that require holding a phone in front of one's face or standing in front of a laptop in good lighting, a wearable glove enables hands-free, natural signing in any environment.

SLIDE 03 / 06 • HARDWARE & SENSING

Wearable Sensing Architecture

Fusing 5 resistive flex sensors with a 6-DOF IMU to capture both finger curvature and 3D hand orientation at 50Hz.

✋ 5-Finger Flex Sensors

Embedded conductive strip sensors along each finger measure joint curvature angles from 0° (flat) to 90° (fully flexed).

🧭 6-DOF IMU (Accel + Gyro)

Wrist-mounted sensor tracking 3D spatial velocity, roll, pitch, and yaw for dynamic sign recognition.

⚡ ESP32 Edge MCU

32-bit dual-core microcontroller digitizing analog voltages and packaging telemetry data packets.

📡 Bluetooth Low Energy

Low-latency, energy-efficient wireless streaming directly to companion mobile devices or web applications.

SLIDE 04 / 06 • AI & NLP PIPELINE

From Sensor Streams to Fluent Speech

Multi-stage pipeline: Signal Filtering → Spatial-Temporal Classification → NLP Grammar Transformation → Text-to-Speech.

🎛️

Signal Preprocessing

Butterworth digital low-pass filtering and dynamic min-max calibration baseline normalization.

🧠

Temporal AI Classifier

Random Forest & SVM for static handshapes; LSTM & Transformers for dynamic multi-frame trajectory signs.

🗣️

NLP Sentence Synthesis

Translates concatenated sign glosses (e.g. [I] [WANT] [WATER]) into polite, fluent spoken English ("I want water, please.").

SLIDE 05 / 06 • IMPACT & APPLICATIONS
