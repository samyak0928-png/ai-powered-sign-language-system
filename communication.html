LINGUISTIC INTELLIGENCE

Why an NLP Layer Is Essential for Sign Language

Sign languages are complete natural languages with independent grammar rules that differ fundamentally from spoken English.

🔄

Syntax & Word Order

Sign languages often utilize Topic-Comment or Object-Subject-Verb structures. A signer performing [DOCTOR] [I] [NEED] requires grammatical inversion to produce the natural English sentence "I need a doctor."

✨

Politeness & Context Markers

In spoken conversation, function words (articles, prepositions, politeness honorifics) are essential. The NLP layer enriches raw sign gloss sequences into polite, socially context-appropriate vocalizations.

🗣️

Acoustic Autonomy

By converting sign sequences directly into natural acoustic speech, the signer can communicate with cashiers, doctors, and strangers without requiring the recipient to look away from their work to read a screen.
