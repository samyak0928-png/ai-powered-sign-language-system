Layer 1: User Gesture (Sign Input)

Physical Input

The signer performs standard or calibrated sign language gestures. The forward directional system captures physical hand postures without requiring the signer to look at a screen or hold a phone.

USER ADAPTATION

Every Hand Is Different: 3-Step Calibration

Hand geometry, skin elasticity, sensor alignment, and signing speed vary per individual. A planned 3-step calibration normalizes individual baseline variances.

01

Calibrate

The user performs 3 baseline gestures (open palm, flat rest, tight fist) to record anatomical minimum and maximum resistance bounds.

02

Normalize

Raw voltage telemetry is dynamically mapped onto a standardized 0.0–1.0 range using user-specific min-max calibration coefficients.

03

Personalize

Fine-tuning weights adapt to the signer's individual movement cadence, reducing false negatives and maintaining high recognition confidence.

ENGINEERING TRADEOFFS

Smart Glove Sensing vs. Camera Computer Vision

An objective engineering analysis of wearable sensors versus optical vision.

Evaluation Dimension Smart Glove Sensing (This Concept) Camera / Computer Vision Alone
Lighting Independence 100% immune to dark rooms, direct sunlight, or shadows Prone to degradation in low-light or uneven illumination
Occlusion & Line-of-Sight Direct physical contact; finger flexion always measured Fingers occluded behind palms or bodies are lost by single camera
Privacy & Mobility No video streaming required; sign while walking or outdoors Requires pointing camera at user; raises privacy concerns in public
Facial & Torso Grammar Glove alone does not measure eyebrows or facial cues Naturally captures facial expressions and head nods
Wearable Convenience Requires donning a physical glove Zero wearables required; uses standard smartphone camera
⚡

Long-Term Ideal: Glove + Camera Sensor Fusion

The optimal future architecture combines glove-based finger bending & IMU dynamics with lightweight camera-based facial expression tracking for comprehensive natural sign language comprehension.
